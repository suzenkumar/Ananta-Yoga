About Ananta Yoga

Tradition-rooted yoga, built for real life in Balasore

Ananta Yoga helps students get the best of health through routine yogic practice — asana, pranayama and meditation — improving strength, balance and flexibility. It also supports back pain relief, eases arthritis symptoms, benefits heart health, and helps manage stress, PCOD, PCOS and hormonal imbalance.

We connect every student with a supportive community and better self-care, running programs for basic fitness, weight loss, medical cases, and pre- and post-natal care with diet plans. Our students span school and college students, office-goers, homemakers and senior citizens.
