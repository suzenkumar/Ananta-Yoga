Welcome

A journal for the practice, on and off the mat.

This is where we keep the small moments — a class that ran long because no one wanted to leave savasana, a garland strung for Yoga Day, a stretch taken at a highway rest stop. We write about postures and breath, but just as often about the everyday habits that make a calmer life possible.
