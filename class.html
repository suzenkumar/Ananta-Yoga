Our Classes

A range of practice, rooted in tradition

At Ananta Yoga (Yoga For Life), we offer classes designed for every level — from beginners to advanced practitioners — to promote physical wellness, mental clarity and emotional balance.

01

Traditional Yoga

A holistic, 360° approach to health and wellness — physical, mental, emotional and spiritual — creating harmony among mind, body and soul.

Join Now →
02

Hatha Yoga

Focused on physical postures (asanas) and breath control (pranayama) to balance body, mind and spirit — slow, deliberate movement building flexibility and calm.

Join Now →
03

Rubber Neti (Kriya)

Also known as Sutra Neti — a yogic cleansing practice using a rubber tube to clear the nasal tract, remove blockages and mucus, and improve nasal hygiene.

Join Now →
04

Pranayam

A breathing practice that regulates the flow of prana, or life force, through controlled inhalation, exhalation and breath retention — connecting body and mind.

Join Now →
05

Power Yoga

A physically intense style of Vinyasa yoga building strength, flexibility and endurance through flowing sequences and synchronized breathing.

Join Now →
06

Surya Namaskar

Sun Salutation — a sequence of 12 linked asanas traditionally performed to greet the sun, deeply rooted in Indian culture as a gesture of gratitude for its life-sustaining energy.

Join Now →
07

Meditation

Focusing or clearing the mind to reach calm and clarity, using breath, mantra or visualization — reducing stress and anxiety while improving focus.

Join Now →
08

Kid's Yoga

A playful introduction to yoga for children — storytelling, games, music and imagination build strength, balance, coordination and confidence. No experience needed.
